Artifact Magic Box: A small chunky carved wooden music box with a hinged lid, painted deep barn red with aged brass corners and a small brass crank handle on its right side, on the front of the box a straight row of six big round wooden buttons painted red, green, white, black, blue and yellow, slightly three-quarter view from the front and above
Artifact A Nice Lute: A small lute with a round pear-shaped body made of pale curved antlers joined together, a short neck with a bent-back pegbox, four taut strings, a small round sound hole, hand-carved and painted warm ochre and cream, three-quarter view.
Artifact Cactus Heart: A heart-shaped carved wooden cactus, painted muted teal green with rows of tiny cream thorns, a small pink flower blooming on its top, sitting in a tiny chipped terracotta pot, a faint warm glow painted at its centre.
Artifact Ever Thirsting: A cracked dry hollow wooden water gourd with a wooden stopper hanging open on a string, painted dusty ochre with a web of dry cracks, a single tiny dried drop painted at the bottom, a few grains of sand spilling from its mouth.
Artifact Blanket: A thick folded woollen blanket carved from wood and painted in chunky red and cream checks with a deep walnut-brown fringe, a small neat folded stack with one corner turned over, a patched square sewn on.
Artifact Sake Bottle: A tall slim ceramic-looking bottle with a narrow neck and a small round wooden cork, painted off-white with a single brush-stroke band of indigo blue around its middle, plus a tiny matching round cup beside it.
Artifact Family Picture: A small wooden picture frame with a plain carved border painted dark walnut and brass, inside it a faded old family photograph of three standing figures: a tall father, a mother and a small boy, each face scratched out and blacked over with heavy dark ink, corner slightly torn.
