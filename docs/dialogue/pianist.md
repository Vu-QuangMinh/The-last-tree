# The Pianist: the whole fight, as a script (2026-11-08)

**P** = The Pianist (Ludwig) · **You** = the player (what you pick) · *narration* = text in italics on screen · [stage] = what happens on screen (music, light, animation).

## 1. The stage

[stage] The curtain opens on a dark stage. A grand piano, one spotlight. Beside the piano, a small sign on a stand: **"Silence, please."**

*A tall, thin man in a black suit and a hat sits at a grand piano. Where his left hand should be, the sleeve is pinned up. A small sign beside the piano reads: Silence, please.*

He does not speak, at any point of the talk.

---

## 2. The talk

None of the questions costs Essence. Ask one and it disappears from the list; the others stay. **Engage!** is always there.

### Your options

1. **You:** Who are you?
   [stage] He does not look up. He raises his hand and taps the **Silence, please** sign, twice.

2. **You:** Can you let me go through?
   [stage] He taps the **Silence, please** sign.

3. **You:** Why are you here?
   [stage] He taps the **Silence, please** sign.

4. **You:** Where is your arm?
   [stage] He taps the **Silence, please** sign. *(draft: a beat longer than the others, his hand rests on the sign before it drops.)*

5. **Engage!** → the normal fight (section 3A).

### Only if you carry the Mysterious Hand (from the Handyman)

6. **You:** I think this is your hand.
   *The Pianist nods briefly.*

   Two new options replace the list (Engage! stays):

   6a. **You:** *Give his hand back.*
       [stage] The Mysterious Hand leaves your bag and turns into a **ball of light**. It floats across the stage and settles on his **left shoulder**. The light fades: the hand is there, whole, at the end of his sleeve.
       *(draft)* *He flexes the fingers once, slowly, as if remembering them.*
       → the fight with **Für Elise** (section 3B). The Mysterious Hand is gone from your artifacts.

   6b. **You:** *Keep his hand.*
       *(draft)* *He looks at the hand a moment longer, then turns back to the keys.*
       → the normal fight (section 3A). You keep the Mysterious Hand.

## 3. The fight

He never attacks. From the moment the fight starts he plays one piece, in real time, through every turn and every animation; his arm moves back and forth along the keys with the music. If he reaches the end of the piece, he slams every key, **"The End"** comes up, and you die.

He has 50 Essence, as **five pages of 10**. Each time a page is cleared, he stops the music and turns the page (his turn: you can do nothing, and every status on him is gone), then plays on from where he stopped. When the fifth page is cleared, he is beaten.

### 3A. The normal fight
- He plays **Turkish March** (Mozart) with one hand and one foot.
- Win: you get the **Turkish March** score (artifact: every time an Extension happens, apply 1 Burn to a random enemy).

### 3B. After you gave his hand back
- He plays **Für Elise** instead (slowed, with reverb), with both hands.
- Win: you get the **Für Elise** score (artifact: every time an Extension happens, heal 2). This is the only way to get it.
- The score's picture shows the first bar, **E D# E D# E B D C A**. The first time each of the 6 notes appears, it is coloured like the Magic Box buttons:
  **E green, D# black, B red, D yellow, C blue, A white**. (The same 6 colours, in the same order, that the Magic Box shows when its tune is played right: green, black, red, yellow, blue, white. This is Ludwig's part of the admin code.)

### When he is beaten *(draft)*
- 3A: *He lifts his hand from the keys. The last note rings out over an empty stage.*
- 3B: *He plays the last bar softly, with both hands, and does not slam the keys. Then he is gone, and the score is left on the piano.*
