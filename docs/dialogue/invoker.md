# The Invoker: pre-fight conversation

Same rules as the Handyman's (see handyman.md): before the fight only the boss, the background and your bag; 3
questions (each costs a specific Essence from your starting bag) + Engage!; each opens 3 more, each of those 0-3 more;
some cost an Essence, some are free; Engage! always there; no going back; an option whose whole branch has been
explored (in any run) gets a green tick. Lines are proofread (spelling and grammar), keeping the user's voice.

The boss's name shows as "???" until you have asked "Who are you?" (in any run).

Notation: `[cost]` is the Essence the option takes (free if none).

In the fight's second phase, each word he says (Quas / Wex / Exort) frees that Essence in his spells: it stays on
the card as a glowing orb, but no longer needs to be chanted.

Opening (narration): *A tall man in a ragged robe blocks the road.*

## The tree

- **Who are you?** [Water]  *(complete)*
  - Invoker: "I... am the Invoker: the Pinnacle of Wizards and Wizardry, the Master of the Elements."
  - **The Master of the Elements? Prove it.** [Fire]
    - Invoker: "Name any element."
    - **Fire.** [2 Fire]
    - **Water.** [2 Water]
    - **Air.** [2 Air]
    - **Effect (each):** you get that element's Lens (Flame / Tide / Gale Lens), or its upgrade if you already have it.
      If you already have the upgraded Lens: Invoker: "You are a master of this element as well, I see." and the 2
      Essence come back.
    - **Then (whatever happened):** Invoker: "And now you can witness the Master of the Elements in action, mortal."
      The fight starts.
  - **Who taught you magic?** [Water]
    - Invoker: "Teachers, I have had many, but every one of them was destroyed, for a fatal mistake they all
      committed."
    - **What was their mistake?** [Air]
      - Invoker: "They tried to stop me from obtaining the essence of Space and Time, from transcending Fate."
    - **Are you going to destroy me too?** [free]
      - Invoker: "But of course. If not by the wish of Fate, then for my own amusement."
  - **Are you always this humble?** [free]
    - Invoker: "You dare mock the Arch Wizard, mortal? I will give you but one chance. Apologize, now."
    - **I'm sorry, O Pinnacle of Wizardry.** [free]
      - Invoker: "Very well, you have my forgiveness."
      - **Effect:** +3 random Essence.
    - **No.** [free]
      - Invoker: "So you have chosen... death."
      - **Effect:** the fight starts at once.
    - **\*Laugh.\*** [free]
      - Invoker: "Inconceivable insolence! Behold: Wex Wex Wex!" *A huge electrical explosion.*
      - **Effect:** you lose every Essence in your bag and all of next turn's; the fight starts.
- **What do you want?** [Fire]  *(complete)*
  - Invoker: "To transcend my own fate and become the master of this universe."
  - **What do you mean, "transcend your own fate"?** [Air]
    - Invoker: "A curious bug, aren't you, mortal? Very well. Seeing as you will be destroyed momentarily, I will
      entertain one of your questions."
    - **How exactly does one escape Fate?** [Air]
      - Invoker: "You cannot escape Fate, for He is the creator of this universe. You can only hope to defeat Him
        and become the new Fate. Only then will you be your own master."
    - **What happens to everyone else's fate?** [Water]
      - Invoker: "I would be in control of their fates."
    - **What's your favourite colour?** [free]
      - Invoker: "The colour of my hair: golden, touched with silver, shining like the trees of a faraway land.
        Here, take one."
      - **Effect:** you get **Hair of the Fairest** (see below).
  - **What would you do as master of the universe?** [Water]
    - Invoker: "I will leave that to the wisdom of my future self. Only from the summit of the mountain can one see
      far, and from there, my wisdom will know no bounds."
    - **What if your future self is a fool?** [free]
      - Invoker: "Your current self is a fool, for you have chosen death."
      - **Effect:** the fight starts at once.
    - **And if you never reach the summit?** [Fire]
      - Invoker: "I am immortal, fool. Can you even conceive of it? 'Never' is not a word for an immortal. With
        boundless time comes boundless knowledge. Here, have a glimpse of immortality."
      - **Effect:** you take 10 damage and gain 2 random **useless spells** (see below).
  - **Isn't mastering the elements enough for you?** [free]
    - Invoker: "It is never enough."
    - **What would be enough?** [free]
      - Invoker: "Nothing. I was created this way. But our fight will be a worthy distraction for this endless life
        of mine."
    - **That sounds lonely.** [Water]
      - Invoker: "It... is lonely. This thirst of mine can never be quenched. Perhaps, if I were master of my own
        soul, I could finally MAKE it be satisfied. You are kind, mortal. I shall bestow my wisdom upon you."
      - **Effect:** you get 3 of his spells (random).
- **Why are you here?** [Air]  *(complete)*
  - Invoker: "To stop mortals like you from walking the Path."
  - **What is the Path?** [Fire]
    - Invoker: "The Path is the only path you could ever take. It matters not which way you turn or where you
      choose to stop; you are compelled to walk it."
    - **Where does the Path lead?** [Air]
      - Invoker: "To the beginning and the end of everything. To the Hands of Fate, but not to Fate Himself."
    - **If I'm compelled to walk it, why try to stop me?** [free]
      - Invoker: "As you are compelled to walk it, I am compelled to stop you. It has been written in our fates."
    - **Do you walk a Path too?** [Water]
      - Invoker: "I once walked a different Path, but the master of this universe brought me here and trapped me
        like a caged animal. But worry not, mortal, for soon I will transcend my own fate and bring this universe
        under my command."
  - **Who walked the Path before me?** [Water]
    - Invoker: "No one but you, in all conceivable universes. You alone can walk this Path."
    - **Why me?** [Fire]
      - Invoker: "It was the will of Fate. Maybe the answer you seek lies at the end of this Path. Or maybe your
        journey ends here." *Fire, water and ice shards begin to swirl around his fingers.*
    - **Then who are the ones who came back different?** [Water]
      - *Only offered once you have heard the Handyman say "they all came back different" (in any run).*
      - Invoker: "You. A newly reborn you, with more knowledge and purpose. There has only ever been one traveller
        on this Path."
    - **That's a lot of pressure.** [free]
      - Invoker: "Let me end your worries once and for all, mortal." *Fire, water and ice shards begin to swirl
        around his fingers.*
  - **Can I just take the scenic route?** [free]
    - Invoker: "Your feeble wit amuses me not. I will entertain one last question before I turn you to dust."
    - **What happens to me after the dust?** [Air]
      - Invoker: "I applaud your foolish bravery in the face of great danger. Here, let us enjoy this occasion."
      - *With a chant from a tongue long lost to time, the Invoker expands your mind and his.*
      - **Effect:** he conjures 2 more spells each turn; you choose 2 more spells for this fight (your spellbook
        opens: pick 2, press OK, and the fight starts).
    - **Why do you call everyone "mortal"?** [free]
      - Invoker: "For I am the immortal Invoker, and your insignificant existence does not deserve a name."
    - **Could you turn me into gold dust instead?** [free]
      - Invoker: "Certainly," *he said, with a twisted grin.*
      - **Effect:** the fight starts at once; his first spells are Cold Snap, Tornado, Chaos Meteor and Deafening
        Blast, and he casts all four on his first turn, whatever you chant.
- **Engage!**

## Rewards and effects from the conversation

- **Lens** ("Prove it." -> Fire / Water / Air, 2 of that Essence): that element's Lens, or its upgrade; already
  upgraded: "You are a master of this element as well, I see." and the 2 Essence come back. Then the fight starts.
- **+3 random Essence** ("I'm sorry, O Pinnacle of Wizardry.").
- **Fight at once** ("No." / "What if your future self is a fool?").
- **Wex Wex Wex** ("\*Laugh.\*"): you lose every Essence in your bag and all of next turn's; the fight starts.
- **Hair of the Fairest** (artifact; "What's your favourite colour?"): 1% Refund for any Essence each chant (tiny on
  purpose: its real worth is the free shop).
  - At a merchant, the shopkeeper asks for it: *The shopkeeper seems restless. You ask what's wrong.* "Nothing, my
    friend," says the shopkeeper, bowing low and stammering. "Nothing... unless I may be so bold as to speak of the
    strand of hair in your bag. It outshines every coin in my till, every gem in my cases. I would never ask for such
    a gift. But you did ask what I wanted." Options: **Give him the Hair of the Fairest** (everything in his shop is
    free this visit) / **Refuse**. *(approved)*
- **A glimpse of immortality** ("And if you never reach the summit?"): 10 damage, and all 3 **Forbidden Knowledge**
  spells:
  - Three spells, all named **Forbidden Knowledge**: patterns F F F F F F, W W W W W W and A A A A A A. Rarity: a new
    one, **Unique**. Fleeting. Card text: "It does nothing."
  - Casting one does nothing, and it is gone for good (meta progression: never given again, in any run). The line
    it shows depends on how many you have cast so far (across runs):
    1. "Nothing happened. You have been fooled."
    2. "Nothing happened again. Have you not learned, mortal?"
    3. "Fool you thrice: shame on whom?"
  - **The Codex page "Forbidden Knowledge"** is there from the start, all in unreadable symbols. Each cast turns more
    of it into text; after the 3rd it reads: "The Hands of Fate is merely a tool, a servant to Fate Himself.
    Defeating him would but reset your progress, unless you have obtained the essences of Time, Space and
    Perfection. To gather them, you mus..." and the rest of the page stays symbols.
- **A mind expanded** ("What happens to me after the dust?"): "With a chant from a tongue long lost to time, the
  Invoker expands your mind and his." He conjures 5 spells each turn instead of 3; your spellbook opens, you choose 2
  more spells for this fight, press OK, and the fight starts.
- **Gold dust** ("Could you turn me into gold dust instead?"): the fight starts; his first turn casts Cold Snap,
  Tornado, Chaos Meteor and Deafening Blast, whatever you chant.
- **His wisdom** ("That sounds lonely."): 3 spells of his, for the player (all of them, or 3 at random from a bigger
  set later). Player versions, all with a cooldown:
  - **Tornado**: Freeze all enemies for 1 turn. 5 Essence (A A W A A), Cooldown 2.
  - **Chaos Meteor**: Burn 1 on a random enemy, 5 times. 4 Essence (F F A F), Cooldown 2.
  - **Deafening Blast**: Disarm all enemies (their next attack does nothing) and remove 1 leftmost Essence of each.
    4 Essence (W A F W), Cooldown 2.

## In the game

The tree is in `scripts/core/boss_talk.gd` (BossTalk.TREES.invoker); effects in `Fight.talk_effect` and
`FightScreen._talk` (lens, forbidden_spells, invoker_spells, choose2). Spells: forbidden_f / _w / _a (Unique),
inv_tornado, inv_chaos_meteor, inv_deafening_blast. Artifact: hair_of_the_fairest; the shop item `give_hair`. The
Codex page text: BossTalk.forbidden_text (SaveManager setting "forbidden_cast").
