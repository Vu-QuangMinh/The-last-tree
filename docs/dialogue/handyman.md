# The Handyman: pre-fight conversation

His voice: a kind soul forced to be cruel, hiding the pain behind japes, insults and sarcasm. Simple words, sharp
wit. (Some of his lines were rewritten in that voice on 2026-10-09; the rest are the user's originals.)

Rules (settled with the user, 2026-10-09):
- His name shows as "???" until you have asked "Who are you?" (in any run). Opening: "A stranger blocks the road,
  flexing more fingers than you can count."
- Before the fight: only the boss, the background and your bag. HP, chant, "coming next" and the log slide in from
  their edges when you Engage.
- Top level: 3 questions (each costs a specific Essence, taken from your starting bag: the fight gets harder) + Engage!
- Each question opens 3 more; each of those 0-3 more. Some cost an Essence, some are free.
- Engage! is always one of the choices. No going back up the tree.
- Answers are mostly background on the characters and the world; the user may add effects later.
- An option whose whole branch has been explored (in any run) shows a green tick at the right end of its box.

Notation: `[cost]` is the Essence the option takes (free if none). Depth: question -> 3 options -> 0-3 options;
after the last level only Engage! is left.

## The tree

- **Who are you?** [Water]  *(complete)*
  - Handyman: "Can't you see? I am the Handyman. I got HANDS."
  - **How many hands have you got, exactly?** [free]
    - Handyman: "Let me count. One, two... At least four. Is that too many? I don't know. I was created like this.
      Why don't you count for yourself?"
    - **\*Count his hands.\*** [free]
      - *You only see 2 hands, but it seems he has more under his coat.*
    - **Who created you?** [Air]
      - Handyman: "The one with a twisted sense of humor. Do you understand how hard life is with so many hands? Of
        course you don't. You got off easy."
    - **"At least"? You don't know how many hands you have?** [free]
      - Handyman: "Who's got lots of thumbs and no brain? THIS guy! Muahahaha."
  - **Where did you get all those hands?** [Fire]
    - Handyman: "From Fate, of course. Where else? Fate dictates everything, from your ugly face to my many
      hands. Hehehehe."
    - **Who is Fate?** [Fire]
      - Handyman: "Wow. Smart AND pretty. Fate made the world, genius. You too. You walk around in it every day and
        never asked? Must be nice under that rock."
    - **Hey! My face is fine!** [free]
      - Handyman: "No, it isn't. Hold still, I'll fix it." *His voice was gentle. The sword he pulled out was not.*
    - **Did Fate send you here?** [Water]
      - Handyman: "The Hands of Fate put me here. They put everything somewhere. Nobody asks where they'd like to go."
  - **Who do you fix things for?** [Water]
    - Handyman: "Fix? Who said fix? These hands don't fix. They BREAK. WAARRGGGHH!!!"

- **What do you want?** [Fire]  *(complete)*

  - **Why are your hands so cruel?** [Fire]

    - **You don't have to be cruel.** [free]

    - **Who decides what kind of soul you get?** [Air]

    - **Then why do you dream of making chairs?** [free]

  - **Have you ever tried to make a chair?** [Air]

    - **Take the chair.** [free]
      - **Effect:** you get the **Deadly Chair**.
      - Handyman: "Enough talking. Time to get cruel."
    - **Refuse the chair.** [free]
      - Handyman: "Enough talking. Time to get cruel."
  - **Maybe I could help you.** [free]
    - Handyman: "Help? Me? Stop pitying me. Who are you to help anybody?"
    - **I am Kind Heart, for my heart is kind. Here is some water for you.** [Water]

    - **Just someone who likes comfy chairs.** [free]
      - Handyman: "Then you came to the wrong place. Don't worry. After this, you might never need to sit again."
    - **Nobody. Forget I said anything.** [free]

- **Why are you here?** [Air]  *(complete)*
  - Handyman: "I am here to stop you from going any further."
  - **Why would you not want to let me go any further?** [Water]
    - Handyman: "It is not what I want. It is my purpose. I have no say in this matter, and neither do you."
    - **Who gave you this purpose?** [Air]
      - Handyman: "My creator. Fate, He who exists beyond time and space."
    - **Everyone has a say. Even you.** [free]
      - Handyman: "Here is my say: Die, fool!"
    - **Then I'm sorry it has to be this way.** [free]
      - Handyman: "The feeling is mutual."
  - **What lies further on?** [Fire]
    - Handyman: "Who knows? I have never travelled this road myself. But those who went... they all came back

    - **Different how?** [Fire]
      - Handyman: "Like they finally knew where they were going."
    - **Who came back?** [Water]
      - Handyman: "The ones who couldn't understand the essence of Time and Space. So, most people."
    - **Then I'll be the first to come back the same.** [free]
      - Handyman: "If you stay the same, you would not come back. If you came back, you would not be the same.

    - **Purpose is overrated.** [free]
      - Handyman: "Agreed, but no one can go against their purpose. Much less you and me."
- **Engage!**

## Rewards and effects from the conversation

- **Deadly Chair** (artifact; "Have you ever tried to make a chair?" -> Take the chair): lose 2 HP at the start of
  each fight.
  - Hidden: at a merchant, the shopkeeper asks "Hey, that is a deadly-looking chair. Would you like me to fix it?"
    with a price tag (100 Leaves). Paying turns it into the **Comfy Chair**: heal 8 HP before each fight. Until then
    its description only mentions the -2 HP.
- **Chair** (artifact; "Maybe I could help you." -> "I am Kind Heart..."): heal 4 HP before each fight.
  - It can't be fixed: it can only be **upgraded** (the merchant's usual artifact upgrade), and its upgrade is the
    **Comfy Chair** (heal 8 before each fight). That it upgrades into the Comfy Chair is hidden until you do it.
- **Kind Heart refund** ("Are you okay? I got some water for you."): every Essence this conversation cost you comes
  back, plus 2 random Essence.
- **Enraged** ("You don't have to be cruel."): the fight starts at once; the Handyman gains +1 Power every time
  you gain Shield.
- **Weaken 2** on the Handyman ("Then why do you dream of making chairs?").

## In the game

The tree lives in `scripts/core/boss_talk.gd` (BossTalk.TREES, ids per option for the green ticks). The talking screen is
`FightScreen._talk()`; the effects are `Fight.talk_pay` / `Fight.talk_effect`; the chairs are artifacts `deadly_chair`,
`chair` (upgrades into the Comfy Chair) and `comfy_chair`; the merchant's fix is the shop item `fix_chair`.
