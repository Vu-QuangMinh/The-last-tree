# The Handyman: every line of dialogue (extracted from scripts/core/boss_talk.gd, 2026-10-18)

**H** = The Handyman · **You** = the player (what you pick) · *narration* = text in italics on screen.
Every option except the first two costs one Essence from your starting bag (cost in brackets: F fire, W water, A air). "Engage!" is always on screen.

*A tall figure with an unsettling smile blocked the road.*

**H:** Hello there, weary traveler. You seem burdened. Would you like me to give you A HAND? Hehe, hehehe.

## Your options

1. **You:** Sure, please give me a hand.
   **H:** How about MORE than just A hand? Muahahahah!
   *He laughs maniacally as his impossibly long arms start to unfold.* → fight

2. **You:** You are creeping me out. Stop that.
   **H:** Thank you for your compliment. Let's see how you feel about this! Muahahahah!
   *He laughs maniacally as his impossibly long arms start to unfold.* → fight

3. **You:** Why are you here? **[F]**
   **H:** Fate put me here to, uhm, fix you up. Hehe, hehe.

   3a. **You:** Alright, let's put an end to that creepy laugh of yours. → fight

   3b. **You:** You mean, like, you're here by accident? By chance? Can you let me go, then? **[A]**
       **H:** Oh no, my Chosen One, it was written. You were destined to walk this Path and be... HANDLED by me. Hehe, you will get that soon enough. Hehehehe.

       3b-i. **You:** Show me.
             **H:** With pleasure! Muahahahah!
             *He laughs maniacally as his impossibly long arms start to unfold.* → fight

       3b-ii. **You:** OK, so there's something special about your hands, isn't there? Mind telling me what it is? **[W]**
              **H:** My hands are a gift from God. They are so wonderful. Here, take a look at this one.
              *He unfolds the Tweezer Hand.*
              **H:** This used to belong to a great artist, but it is MINE now. I will show you the rest, in due time. Hahahahaha.
              *He laughs maniacally as his impossibly long arms start to unfold.* → fight. Win it and you get the **Mysterious Hand** artifact (the only way to get it).

## During the fight (log lines, not spoken)

- The Handyman reaches out with two more hands! (phase 2: Hammer and Crossbow)
- Two last hands, a spear and a shield: the Handyman makes his last stand! (phase 3)
- Out of hands, the Handyman shows himself!
- Every hand goes limp for good.
- (Mysterious Hand artifact) The Mysterious Hand steals a <Essence> from <enemy>.

His in-game description (Codex): "A tinkerer with too many hands and a tool in each. He never shows himself until there's nothing left to hide behind."
