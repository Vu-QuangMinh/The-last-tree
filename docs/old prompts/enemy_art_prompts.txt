THE LAST TREE — NORMAL ENEMY ART PROMPTS
==========================================
Prompts written in English (best results with most AI image tools).
Vietnamese enemy name is given only as a label for your own reference.

HOW TO USE
----------
Each entry below is a full, ready-to-paste prompt. Append the SHARED STYLE
block to the end of any prompt you use, so all 23 characters stay visually
consistent as one cast. A shared NEGATIVE PROMPT is also provided for tools
that support one (Stable Diffusion, etc.) — skip it for tools that don't
(Midjourney, DALL-E).

SHARED STYLE (append to every prompt)
--------------------------------------
, dark fantasy creature concept art, painterly digital illustration, thick
clean outlines with soft painterly shading, rich atmospheric rim lighting,
semi-stylized proportions (not photorealistic), full-body centered portrait,
plain dark charcoal-green background, isolated single character, game asset
concept art, highly detailed, trending on artstation --ar 1:1

SHARED NEGATIVE PROMPT
------------------------
photorealistic, photograph, blurry, extra limbs, text, watermark, signature,
cropped, multiple characters, busy background, low detail, deformed anatomy

1. Ashling (Việt: Đóm Lửa)
Elements: Fire-dominant, water accent (FFW)
A small humanoid campfire spirit the size of a child, made of living ember
and dancing flame, dark charcoal skin cracked with glowing orange-red fire
seeping out through the cracks like magma, a faint wisp of blue steam curling
off one shoulder where water clings to it, no visible eyes, just two bright
white ember pinpricks, a mischievous crouched posture like it's about to
lunge and bite, small clawed hands, tiny flickering flame tongues around its
mouth, floating embers drifting around its body.

2. Puddle Slime (Việt: Vũng Nhớt)
Elements: Water-dominant, air accent (WAW)
A translucent teal-blue gelatinous blob creature, roughly knee-height, semi
see-through body sloshing and rippling like a living puddle, tiny bubbles of
trapped air drifting inside it, a simple wide lazy smiling face pressed
against the front of its jelly body, thin trickles of water constantly
dripping and reforming at its base, glossy wet highlight along its rounded
top, sitting in a small shallow puddle of its own leaked fluid on the ground.

3. Gale Sprite (Việt: Yêu Tinh Gió)
Elements: Air-dominant, fire accent (AFA)
A small winged sprite made of swirling pale cyan-white wind, two large
translucent wing-like air vortices instead of arms, a slender wispy body
that fades into mist at the edges, faint ember-orange sparks crackling where
static electricity builds along its wingtips, sharp narrow eyes, caught in a
mid-air lunging pose with a blur of speed lines showing a quick double
strike, tiny dust and leaves swirling around it.

4. Cinder Hound (Việt: Chó Lửa)
Elements: Pure fire (FFF)
A muscular hound the size of a large wolf, entirely made of black obsidian-
like charred hide with deep glowing orange-red fissures running along its
back and legs like cooling lava, a mane of small living flames instead of
fur along its neck, glowing amber eyes, bared teeth wreathed in flame,
heat-shimmer distortion rising off its whole body, low aggressive stance
like it's about to charge, faint smoke trailing from its nostrils.

5. Stone Knight (Việt: Hiệp Sĩ Đá)
Elements: Fire-dominant, water accent (FFW)
A squat armored knight built like a walking boulder, thick cracked grey
stone plate armor fused to its body, molten orange-red fire glowing through
deep cracks in the stone chestplate, a faint sheen of condensation and damp
moss on the lower legs, no visible face inside a heavy blank stone helmet
except two glowing ember slits, standing in a wide braced defensive stance
with one huge stone fist raised, small chips of rock floating around it.

6. Tidecaller (Việt: Gọi Triều)
Elements: Water/air balanced (WAWA)
A slender aquatic humanoid figure made of flowing translucent seawater held
together by swirling currents of pale mist-white air, a flowing cape-like
trail of water that never touches the ground, glowing teal eyes with no
pupils, long thin water-tendril hair that moves like it's underwater even in
open air, hands raised in a summoning gesture with small whirlpools forming
at its fingertips, calm serene expression despite the churning water around
it.

7. Mirror Wisp (Việt: Ma Gương)
Elements: Balanced tri-elemental (AWF)
A small floating humanoid-shaped wisp split into three shifting facets, one
third of its body wreathed in pale wind-mist, one third rippling like liquid
water, one third flickering with tiny embers, its form constantly reshuffles
and never looks quite symmetrical, a fractured mirror-shard mask for a face
reflecting fragmented light in three different colors, a playful unsettling
tilted pose, faint trailing afterimages showing it mid-shuffle.

8. Frost Hex (Việt: Bùa Băng)
Elements: Water-dominant, air accent (WWA)
A small hooded witch-like spirit made of pale blue ice and frost, cracked
translucent ice-skin with frozen mist leaking from the sleeves of a tattered
frayed cloak, thin skeletal ice-blue fingers pointed forward casting a curse,
a hood pulled low hiding all but two glowing cold white eyes, sharp icicles
hanging from its elbows and hood, frozen breath visible in the cold air
around it, faint snowflakes drifting from its cloak.

9. Pickpocket Imp (Việt: Yêu Móc Túi)
Elements: Air-dominant, fire accent (AFA)
A small grinning goblin-like imp with thin windswept grey-lavender skin and
faint ember-orange markings along its arms, oversized mischievous yellow
eyes, wrapped in a patchwork of stolen glowing elemental trinkets and
glowing charms hanging off a ragged belt, crouched in a sneaky thieving
pose with one clawed hand extended mid-grab, small bat-like wind-vane ears,
a satisfied smirk, faint dust devil swirling at its feet.

10. Splitter Ooze (Việt: Nhớt Phân Tách)
Elements: Water/fire balanced (WWFF)
A larger unstable gelatinous blob creature, half of its body a churning
teal-blue water jelly and the other half a simmering orange-red ember jelly,
a visible seam running down its middle where the two halves strain against
each other as if it might tear apart, two lazy mismatched eyes on each half,
faint steam hissing where the fire and water sides meet, dripping and
bubbling texture all over its unstable surface.

11. Overgrowth Vine (Việt: Dây Leo Bùng Phát)
Elements: Air-dominant, fire+water accents (FAWA)
A humanoid figure completely woven from living thorned vines and dry leaves
that rustle in a constant swirl of wind, faint fire-orange buds and small
water droplets clinging to different parts of the vine-body, glowing amber
eyes peering out from a tangle of leaves where a face would be, roots
trailing from its feet still half-planted in the ground, arms ending in
clusters of thorny bramble instead of hands, an air of patient menace.

12. Toll Keeper (Việt: Người Giữ Lộ Phí)
Elements: Water-dominant (WWAW)
A tall gaunt robed figure like a tollbooth ghost, deep blue-grey water-
soaked robes that hang heavy and dripping, a faint swirl of pale air mist
around its shoulders, an old rusted brass scale or ledger chained to its
belt, a featureless hood with only a faint watery glow where a face should
be, one long bony hand extended palm-up as if demanding payment, standing
unnervingly still.

13. Hexer (Việt: Người Nguyền)
Elements: Balanced tri-elemental (FWA)
A hunched cloaked witch doctor figure wrapped in a ragged patchwork robe
stitched from fire-scorched cloth, water-stained leather, and windswept
feathers, one elemental symbol tattoo of each type (flame, droplet, wind
spiral) glowing faintly on its exposed forearms, a necklace of small cursed
charms, narrow glowing eyes under a ragged hood, one hand raised mid-hex
with glowing threads of curse-energy trailing from its fingers toward an
unseen target.

14. Shrine Maiden (Việt: Nữ Tế Đền)
Elements: Water-dominant, air accent (WAW)
A serene robed priestess figure in flowing pale teal-white ceremonial robes
that ripple like calm water, faint wisps of air spiraling around a small
offering bowl she holds in both hands, a gentle closed-eyed devout
expression, long water-slicked hair, soft glowing halo-like ring of water
droplets suspended around her head, standing in a peaceful blessing pose
that feels oddly indifferent to anyone outside her circle.

15. Hush Moth (Việt: Bướm Lặng Câm)
Elements: Air-dominant, water accent (AAW)
A large pale moth-like creature with huge soft wings made of semi-
translucent windswept mist patterned with faint water-drop markings like
eyespots, a small delicate insectoid body, fine feathery antennae, wings
mid-flutter kicking up faint dust motes and silencing ripples, an eerie
quiet stillness to its pose despite the motion of its wings, muted
moon-pale coloring.

16. Blinding Beetle (Việt: Bọ Cánh Chói)
Elements: Fire-dominant, air accent (FAF)
A large armored beetle creature with a glossy carapace of deep ember-red
chitin plating, faint wind-swept dust patterns along its shell, oversized
bulbous glowing white-hot eyes on its head like twin miniature suns about to
flash, short powerful digging legs, small jets of hot air puffing from vents
on its back, a defensive hunched posture with its head lowered and eyes
flaring bright.

17. Leech Bat (Việt: Dơi Hút Máu)
Elements: Balanced tri-elemental (WFA)
A small gaunt bat creature with leathery wind-torn wings, damp water-slicked
fur, and faint ember-orange veins glowing visibly under its thin skin, sharp
needle-like fangs stained dark, hanging in an upside-down perch pose or
mid-swoop with wings spread wide, beady red eyes, a hungry twitchy
expression, small droplets of blood-tinted water dripping from its jaw.

18. Inverter (Việt: Kẻ Đảo Nghịch)
Elements: Fire/water balanced (FWFW)
A tall slender humanoid split perfectly down the middle: one half made of
churning orange-red flame, the other half made of flowing teal-blue water,
each half hissing faintly where they meet at the seam down the center of its
face and body, mismatched glowing eyes (one ember-orange, one water-teal),
arms raised in a mirrored inverting gesture, faint distortion in the air
around it as if reality is swapping at its touch.

19. Warded Golem (Việt: Golem Được Trấn)
Elements: Fully balanced tri-elemental (FFWWAA)
A massive hulking golem constructed from three fused elemental cores
embedded in a heavy stone-grey armored body: a glowing ember core in its
chest, a glowing water core in one shoulder, a glowing air-mist core in the
other, faint magical warding runes etched across its armored plating that
glow softly, no face, just a smooth blank helm with a single warding sigil,
standing immovable in a wide defensive stance, small protective glyphs
orbiting slowly around it.

20. Echo Wraith (Việt: Vong Hồn Vọng Âm)
Elements: Air-dominant (AWAF)
A ghostly translucent wraith figure that appears slightly doubled or
smeared, as if two faint afterimages of itself are always trailing a half-
second behind its real position, pale windswept grey-blue tattered robes
that fray into mist at the edges, faint ember and water motes drifting
through its ethereal body, hollow glowing white eyes duplicated faintly in
the afterimage, an unsettling repeating stutter to its silhouette.

21. Mimic Chest (Việt: Rương Giả Dạng)
Elements: Fire-dominant (FWAF)
An ornate old wooden treasure chest with a face-like carved pattern on its
lid resembling twisted, mismatched features, its lid slightly ajar like a
mouth, rows of small jagged teeth-like metal studs lining the opening,
faint ember-orange glow leaking from inside along with wisps of water vapor
and air, brass corners scorched and dented, sitting deceptively still among
normal-looking treasure, one small clawed foot peeking out from underneath.

22. Last Gasp Spore (Việt: Bào Tử Hơi Tàn)
Elements: Water-dominant, air accent (AWW)
A small bulbous mushroom-like creature with a damp, water-slicked cap
covered in faintly glowing spore pores, thin wispy tendrils of air-borne
spore dust constantly drifting off its cap, short stubby legs, a droopy
sickly-looking but faintly smug expression, its cap slightly swollen and
straining as if about to burst, a sense of fragile menace.

23. Storm Imp (Việt: Yêu Bão)
Elements: Air-dominant, fire accent (AAF)
A small crackling imp made of compressed storm-wind with faint ember sparks
arcing across its translucent windy body like static discharge, a lithe
blurred silhouette that seems to flicker in and out of visibility, jagged
lightning-bolt shaped limbs, glowing yellow-white eyes, caught mid-flicker
with a triple afterimage showing three rapid strikes, small crackling arcs
of electricity dancing between its fingertips.
