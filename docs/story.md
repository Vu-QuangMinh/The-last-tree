# The Last Tower: Lore Bible

The hidden truth. Players see only fragments of this.
Canon comes from the user. Lines marked **(?)** are open questions, not canon.

## 1. The world

1. **V, the Outside God**, created the universe for their own entertainment. V lives outside it.
2. V placed the **Avatar** inside it, also called **the Hands of Fate** or **the Herald**. The Avatar is the source code: it runs everything and makes sure everything goes as intended. Its purpose is to keep V entertained.
3. Every character has sentience, but none may **break character**. Those who do are punished by the Hands. (The Anxious Armadillo shows it: beaten, he begs to stop, tries to hop off the stage, and his string yanks him up and away.)
4. The world is a **puppet theatre**: everyone hangs on strings.

## 2. The Cut Strings (the first rebellion)

### The four
The rebels called themselves **the Cut Strings**. The Hands call them **the Sinners** (Isaac is "the First Sinner").
- **Nila the Darkblade**, best friend of Princess Elise.
- **Isaac**, the merchant's son.
- **Ludwig the Pianist** (formerly Jaeler). He and Elise love each other.
- **Princess Elise.**

### The code
They obtained the **admin code** to the source code. Knowing the Hands would change it if they suspected anything, they feigned ignorance and split it into **4 parts**:
1. **The first half of the digits:** Isaac wrote it on the back of his family photo: *"Dad, don't look for me. But if you do: xxx. Love, Isaac."* (The code never changes. Older note: the back reads "Isaac 093", the digits coloured black, blue, yellow. Which wording is final (?))
2. **The second half of the digits:** Elise told it to her father, King Malfatti, who hid it in his last masterpiece (?).
3. **The colours:** Nila hid them in the **Magic Box** (the Good Ol' Wizard has it now).
4. **The music:** Ludwig hid it in his **secret sheet music** (Für Elise).

### The raid and the betrayal
- The four got very close to the source code, all the way to **Act -1**, and could almost have deleted the Hands.
- **King Malfatti**, Elise's father, hated Ludwig for filling her head with ideas of revolution, and wanted to protect her. He told the Hands the four were coming, in exchange for a promise that Elise would be kept safe and that he would be with her forever. **The Hands laid a trap.**

### The fall
They were caught but **not deleted**: they still serve a purpose, mostly torture, as an example to everyone else and as entertainment for the Hands.
- **Nila** had her eyes and ears removed. She was promised that if she can make it out of there, she will be let go. (Act 0.)
- **Ludwig** had his head and arms removed, but is still allowed upstairs (Act 2). One of his hands became the Handyman's Tweezer Hand (its sleeve is the same black suit cloth as the Pianist's).
- **Isaac** was turned into the worst monster possible (the secret boss of Act 0).
- **Princess Elise** was the only one not caught at once. Ludwig pushed her away from the fight: *"Be free, Elise, my heart and soul. Be free, for me."* She jumped into the source code and **deleted herself**. The Hands can recreate her, but only as a shell; there is no soul left.
- So the Hands kept Malfatti's bargain **to the letter**: Elise is "safe", and he is "with her forever", but only with an empty shell.

### Nivel (was Nivel)
Nivel comes into the story later. He is **Nila's little brother** (and the **Good Ol' Wizard's student**). She had been gone a long while, so he grew worried and went out to find her, and was gone for 273 years. Somehow he got into **Act -1**, all the way to Nila's room, and there he **lies dead in a corner**: she could not see him (her eyes were taken), and she killed him by accident.

## 3. The way down: the admin key and the secret act

- At the end of Act 2 there is always a choice: go **upstairs** (the normal Act 2 boss) or **downstairs**.
- Downstairs asks for the **Admin Key**. The right key opens the secret **Act 0** (Nila, Isaac, and the Hands as its final boss). A wrong key: *"Access denied, transported to the next stage"*, and you start Act 3 instead.
- The key is built from the 4 parts above (2 halves of the digits, the colours, the music). What the final key looks like (6 numbers? numbers and colours?) (?).

## 4. Characters and bosses

### The Merchant
His son Isaac is the First Sinner, locked away below. He wants you to help free him. He has 3 quests (in any order):
- a **blanket** (from the Bramble Witch / Bramble Matron),
- a **chair** (from the Chairman),
- a **bottle** (from Bottle Head).

When all 3 are done he gets drunk and gives you a permanent 10% discount, and you can steal his **family picture**: a dad (him), a wife, and a son deleted from the picture (a black silhouette). Isaac's note and digits are on the back.

### The Handyman (Act 1 boss)
A twisted, sadistic, loyal servant of the Hands. He is rewarded with the hands of characters who acted out of line (the Pianist's hand among them). He enjoys the violence and is proud of his loyalty. Laughs "Hehe, hehehe" and "Muahahahah".
- Talk (in game): *"A tall figure with an unsettling smile blocked the road."* "Hello there, weary traveler. You seem burdened. Would you like me to give you A HAND? Hehe, hehehe."
- The branch **Why are you here? (Fire) > by accident? (Air) > something special about your hands? (Water)** reveals the Tweezer Hand ("This used to belong to a great artist, but it is MINE now."). Winning that fight gives the **Mysterious Hand** and unlocks it for good; it is the only way to get it.

### The Good Ol' Wizard (Act 1 boss; was the Invoker, the Old Wizard, Ol' Wiz)
The first character created for the game, and the oldest. Worried about his student Nivel. He doesn't enjoy the violence but knows there is no way out. One word: **Defeated**. He seems to remember the Chosen One ("ol' friend", "why call me a stranger?") and hushes himself as if someone could hear.
- Talk (in game): *"A troubled man with an impossibly old face sat on the side of the road. He was not aware of your approach."*
- The branch **warmth (Fire) > "just messing with you" (Air) > "tell me where you last saw him" (Water)**: he gives you the **Magic Box** (given at once and unlocked for good). The very first time you ever meet him, "just messing with you" adds: *You lied straight to his face.*
- He thinks Nila hurt Nivel.

### The Masked (Act 1 boss) = King Malfatti
The Masked **is King Malfatti** in disguise, testing the Chosen One. He speaks in verses and riddles (below).

### The Crowned Matador (event, any act): the lore master
A matador with a kingly crown and a golden mask (King Malfatti again (?)). He is the one event that can come more than once a run, and he comes **at least once every run**. He holds out a red cloth with 5 HP and plays a dice game with you (see *The Crowned Matador's game* in section 5). The better you do, the more he tells you: he is the game's lore master, answering one question at a time, and new questions open as you discover things (tracked across runs).

### The Headless Pianist (Act 2 boss) = Ludwig
No head, no hands. He doesn't attack: after the talk he plays through the whole fight. When the song ends he slams every key, "The End" comes up, and you die. 50 HP. The song plays through every turn and animation.
- Normally he plays **The Entertainer** (Joplin, public domain).
- If you bring the **Mysterious Hand**, you may give it back to him: the fight then plays **Für Elise** (slowed, reverb).
- Winning gives that song's score as an artifact (see Artifacts).

### The Skinwalker (Act 2 boss)
No lore yet (?).

### King Malfatti (final boss, Act 3; was Master Penn)
Named after Therese Malfatti, for whom Beethoven may have written Für Elise. Elise's father; he betrayed the four to protect her, and lives with eternal guilt. He tests players to find the right one and passes on the knowledge, but certain things must be done before each part of his knowledge opens (e.g. a line about the merchant only once his quest is started; a line about Nivel only once you have the Magic Box; one about Ludwig once you have the Mysterious Hand). If you win the fight against the Hands, he appears in a cutscene to help you.

### Darklord Zakarim
No longer the final boss. His speech moves to another boss (which one (?)). The speech hides words in capitals:
> YOU, the chosen one, you have come to face your doom. You MUST not have realized how powerful I am. This is the end for you. No HELP would come, for I am THE MERCHANT of death. HE, who control the shadow realm. He who KNOWS no defeat or rival. Your journey end here and that is THE FINAL TRUTH.

### The Anxious Armadillo (Act 2, once ever)
On your very first run, the 2nd fight of Act 2. 20 HP, gains 2 Thorns a turn, never attacks. Beaten, he doesn't break: he turns sad and begs ("Stop, please." / "It hurts." / "I can't take this anymore."), tries to hop off to the right (about 10 s), his string tugs him up ("Oh no." / "Oh @##%#, @$%@ me, #@%$." / "No no no NO!"), then yanks him off the top of the screen, from where he keeps begging ("Please, let me go." / "I won't do it again." / "I swear." / "Please, have some mercy." / "Aaaagrggghrgh!"). Then 5 s of silence and the usual victory. (In game.)

## 6. Artifacts from the story

- **The Bottle** (from Bottle Head): heal 5 whenever you use a bottle.
- **The Blanket** (from the Bramble Witch): Thorn damage is doubled. *"Thick enough to warm a broken heart, but not to mend it."*
- **Mysterious Hand** (Rare; the Handyman's talk): at the start of each fight, steal 1 Essence from a random enemy. *"Once played defiantly, now lies lifeless."* (In game.)
- **Magic Box** (Rare; the Good Ol' Wizard's talk): every 6th spell you cast, Refund its pattern after the Release. *"With the right tune, it may reveal a secret."* In the Codex it can be played (see below). (In game.)
- **The Entertainer** (the Pianist, normal fight): apply 1 Burn to a random enemy per Extension.
- **Für Elise** (the Pianist, after giving back the Mysterious Hand; the secret score): heal 2 per Extension.

### The Magic Box tune (in game: Codex > Artifacts, once the box is unlocked)
Buttons, left to right: red = B, green = E, white = A, black = D#, blue = C, yellow = D.
The first bar of Für Elise (E D# E D# E B D C A) = green, black, green, black, green, red, yellow, blue, white.
Played right, a row of 6 squares appears and stays: the colours in the order the tune first plays them: **green, black, red, yellow, blue, white**.
