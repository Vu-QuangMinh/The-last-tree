# The Last Tower: Lore Bible

The hidden truth. Players see only fragments of this.
Canon comes from the user. Lines marked **(?)** are open questions, not canon.

## True timeline

1. **V, the Outside God**, creates the universe for their own entertainment. V lives outside it.
2. V places the **Avatar** (also called the Hands of Fate or the Herald) inside it. The Avatar is the source code: it runs everything and makes sure everything goes as intended.
3. **The Script:** Nila Darkblade kidnaps the princess of the Kingdom of Happiness. The Dark Lord keeps her at the top of the Last Tower. The hero saves her, and they live happily ever after. The Kingdom is ruled by King Leoric and Queen Leona. Knight Clive helps the hero.
4. The Script loops countless times. The hero dies and respawns, and so does every enemy.
5. **Corruption.** After countless loops, sentience starts to form.
6. **The Invoker** (the Ancient One) wakes first. He understands their situation and knows he's powerless against the universe. Sentience means suffering, so he tries to change the code to erase it. He fails. He spends countless years trying other things, and nothing works.
7. Others wake (fully or partly): Leona, Leoric, Clive, Nila, the Handyman, Alayaya, the Nameless Druid, the Eyeless Seer **(?)**. Many monsters and spirits never wake and still just follow the Script.
8. **Leona's revolt.** She decides the endless cycle of violence has to end. She takes the throne and leads the enemies to camp the hero's respawn point at the gate of the tower. Every time the hero is killed there, he loses his memory and never learns why.
   - The Invoker joins her, though to him it's only a temporary fix.
   - Clive joins her so the hero can never marry the princess, whom Clive loves.
   - Nila, the Script's kidnapper, now works for her.
9. **The game begins.** Leoric breaks the hero out and gives him a version of the story. He doesn't want the hero to know the truth, because if he did, he'd stop doing what has to be done. Nila hunts the hero for the Queen.
10. **Every run:** the hero doesn't die. When he's about to be defeated, Leoric saves him in the nick of time.
11. **First win:** the hero defeats the Dark Lord at the top of the Tower. The princess is lowered down: a piece of cardboard on strings. "You discovered a Princess!" Everyone learns the truth about her.
12. **The King and Queen change (?)** after that.
13. That night, Leoric kidnaps the princess himself and heads to the Tower, meaning to confront the Avatar.
14. Having seen the hero return with the princess, the Invoker teaches the hero what he knows. **The Avatar deletes him** for tampering with the source code. (The player learns only *that* the Avatar deleted him, not why.)
15. **Later runs:** Leoric heroically defeats the Dark Lord, but the Avatar takes control of him. He's now the Big Bad at the top of the Tower. Defeat him and you get the princess back.
16. **Secret final boss: the Avatar.** Unlock method still open (see below).
