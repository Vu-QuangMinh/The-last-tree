F1-B  The Ringmaster's Hand

STYLE (dán vào ô Style / Style of Music):
fast circus galop, cornet, piccolo, trombone, snare, cymbals, calliope, xylophone, whip cracks, 2/4, A minor, 200 BPM, showy, frantic, instrumental

PROMPT (dán vào ô mô tả / Song description):
The ringmaster puppet shows off. Cornets and a piccolo play a fast marching tune, a calliope and xylophone double it, the snare and cymbals drive every beat, and three whip cracks end each phrase before a trumpet fanfare cuts off mid-note. It starts at full tempo on the very first beat, with no intro and no quiet part. Instrumental only. Seamless loop of 2 to 3 minutes, no fade-in, no outro.
