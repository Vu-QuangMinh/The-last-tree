F2-N  Matinee for Ghosts

A honky-tonk piano races with a bouncing left hand, a vibraphone and a xylophone double the runs, the clarinet slides like a ghost, and once per loop everything stops except a projector clatter for one beat. It starts at full tempo on the very first beat, with no intro and no quiet part. Instrumental only. Seamless loop of 2 to 3 minutes, no fade-in, no outro.
