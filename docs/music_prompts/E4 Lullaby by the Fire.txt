E4  Lullaby by the Fire

STYLE (dán vào ô Style / Style of Music):

PROMPT (dán vào ô mô tả / Song description):
A warm little lullaby by a campfire. A harmonium and a soft marimba rock in three, a glockenspiel adds a few twinkles, and the tune stops one note before its end and starts again, as if the player were nodding off. Instrumental only. Seamless loop of 2 to 3 minutes, no fade-in, no outro.
