E1  A Fork in the Path

STYLE (dán vào ô Style / Style of Music):
tiptoeing, toy piano, pizzicato strings, clarinet, marimba, wood block, 5/4, whole-tone, 96 BPM, curious, comic, instrumental

PROMPT (dán vào ô mô tả / Song description):
A puppet tiptoes down a path and cannot decide which way to go. A toy piano and a marimba play two little tunes, one in each ear, that answer each other and never agree. Pizzicato strings and a wood block step on tiptoe, with short silences in between. Instrumental only. Seamless loop of 2 to 3 minutes, no fade-in, no outro.
