F2-E  Backstage Chase

STYLE (dán vào ô Style / Style of Music):

PROMPT (dán vào ô mô tả / Song description):
A puppet is chased through the wings of the theatre. Two guitars strum a hard galloping rhythm, the violin and clarinet trade breakneck runs, a xylophone and wood blocks tap like running feet, and every fourth bar a rimshot slams a door and the chase restarts. It starts at full tempo on the very first beat, with no intro and no quiet part. Instrumental only. Seamless loop of 2 to 3 minutes, no fade-in, no outro.
