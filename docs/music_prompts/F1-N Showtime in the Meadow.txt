F1-N  Showtime in the Meadow

STYLE (dán vào ô Style / Style of Music):

PROMPT (dán vào ô mô tả / Song description):
A hoedown at a puppet show that has gone out of control. A banjo rolls non-stop, the fiddle and tin whistle chase each other, the washboard and a xylophone keep a tight rhythm, and every fourth bar the fiddle calls and the banjo answers a half-step off. It starts at full tempo on the very first beat, with no intro and no quiet part. Instrumental only. Seamless loop of 2 to 3 minutes, no fade-in, no outro.
